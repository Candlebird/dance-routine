Steps

How to lay out your dance

  1. Load your song (button or drag & drop). It's saved in this browser so it'll be here next time.
  2. Find the beat. Type the BPM, or press play and tap T on every beat, starting on a “1”. Nudge “Beat 1 at” until the grid lines sit on the beats.
  3. Fix drifting bars. If the song speeds up or slows down, drag the numbered bar markers (just above the waveform) so each sits on its “1”. The 8 beats inside each bar are spread evenly between markers. Or press B on every “1” while the song plays to tap all the bars in from scratch. Reset bars goes back to the even BPM grid.
  4. Mark the moves. While the song plays, press M each time a new move starts. The previous step ends automatically.
  5. Describe each step in the cards below: a name (“Basic sway”, “Underarm turn”, “Dip!”) and notes on footwork, who leads, and where your hands go.
  6. Add your rehearsal video (🎬 Load video). It plays in sync with the song, without its own sound. Set where it starts and how fast it plays. If you know both tempos, Match tempo sets the speed. For an exact fit, unlink and set two Sync points.
  7. Practice. Slow the speed down, loop a tricky step, and use Practice view for big text you can read from across the room.
